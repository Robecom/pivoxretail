Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Pivox LLC ("Pivox," "we," "us") operates pivoxmarket.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, shipping and billing address, email, phone number and the contents of messages you send.
  • Order information: items purchased, order history and delivery details. Payments are handled by third-party payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Automatic information: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and referring pages, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use information

To process and deliver orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud, improve our site, meet legal obligations and, where permitted, send service or marketing messages you can opt out of at any time.

How we share information

We share information with service providers that help us operate (payment processors, shipping carriers, warehouses, hosting and analytics) and when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep the site working and understand usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No system is completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California and other U.S. states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, request access, correction or deletion, and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact us by email at info@pivoxmarket.com or by mail at the address below. We may need to verify your identity first.

Related pages

See our Cookie Policy and Your Privacy Choices page for more on cookies and your rights.

Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.
