Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Pivox LLC ("Pivox," "we," "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on pivoxretail.com.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device. They can keep a site working, remember preferences, or measure and advertise.

What we use

Our site does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not use tracking pixels to follow you across other sites. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Third-party services

  • Fonts: our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request to Google that includes your IP address.
  • Hosting: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Browsers also let you send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. Because we do not sell your information or use it for targeted advertising, these signals do not change how we handle it.
