About Pivox Retail

Pivox Retail is the online store of Pivox LLC, based in Okemos, Michigan.

We started Pivox Retail to make everyday purchases simple: the pantry staples, snacks and drinks people reorder again and again, from brands they already know, delivered in original packaging.

How we operate

  • Verified sourcing. We buy from authorized and verified suppliers and distributors.
  • Nationwide warehousing. We keep inventory in several U.S. warehouses to shorten delivery times.
  • Straightforward service. Clear pricing, tracked shipping and a 30-day return policy.
