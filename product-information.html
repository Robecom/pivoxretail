Product Information and Recalls

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Always read the label

Product names, sizes and images on this site are provided for identification. Manufacturers change recipes, ingredients, packaging and sizes from time to time, so the label on the package you receive is the most accurate source for ingredients, allergens, nutrition facts and directions.

Allergens

Many foods contain or are made near major allergens such as milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy and sesame. If you have an allergy or dietary restriction, check the full label before eating, and contact the manufacturer with questions. We repackage nothing, so allergen warnings are those of the original manufacturer.

Dating and storage

We aim to ship products with reasonable remaining shelf life. If an item arrives past its best-by or use-by date, contact us and we will replace it or refund it under our Return Policy. Store products as directed on the label.

Recalls

If a manufacturer or a government agency recalls a product we sold, we will contact affected customers by email where we can and offer a refund. You can also check current recalls at fda.gov. If you have a product you believe is unsafe, stop using it and contact us right away.

Not medical advice

Information on this site is not medical or nutritional advice. Talk to a healthcare professional about dietary needs.
